Why People Chase Success

If you pause for a moment and strip life down to its essence, what do you find? For most of human history, survival was the primary goal — food, shelter, safety. But in the modern world, where many basic needs are met, survival is no longer enough. People crave something beyond subsistence. They chase success. But what does that even mean? And why does it matter so much to us? We speak of success as if it’s universal, yet it’s deeply personal. To some, it means wealth. To others, status. To others still, it’s mastery of a craft, or simply raising a family with love. Yet behind all these definitions lies a shared hunger: the desire to matter, to feel that one’s life carries weight. Success is not just about achievement; it’s about significance.
